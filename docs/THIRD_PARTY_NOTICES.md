# Third-party provenance and release status

Public availability of this package does not constitute blanket relicensing of its contents. No new repository-wide license has been assigned.

Numerical helper files in src/subspacead and historical tools originate from the local SubspaceAD-derived research tree. Existing comments and attributions are preserved; core/pca.py credits PCAonGPU. No LICENSE file was found at the local SubspaceAD_original root during packaging. Upstream redistribution permissions and required notices remain to be verified; no license has been selected for author-owned additions. Missing license text does not imply unrestricted permission.

DINOv2 weights and third-party datasets are not redistributed. Obtain these from their owners under their respective terms. Raw images, cached weights, personal files, signed forms and submission correspondence are excluded.

Source hashes and relative provenance are recorded in source_manifest.json. The new launcher is packaging work, not an assertion that all experiments have been rerun with this release.
